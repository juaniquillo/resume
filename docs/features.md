# Resume Manager Features

A detailed walkthrough of the core features and capabilities included in Resume Manager.

## 1. Comprehensive Career Tracking
Manage every aspect of your professional profile in one unified dashboard:
- **Basics**: Personal details, professional summary, contact info, and website links.
- **Location**: Structured address information (city, country code, postal code, region).
- **Profiles**: Social media and professional network links (GitHub, LinkedIn, X, etc.).
- **Work History**: Companies, positions, start/end dates, and descriptions.
- **Education**: Institutions, areas of study, study types, and dates.
- **Skills & Languages**: Proficiency levels and associated keywords/technologies.
- **Volunteer & Awards**: Community contributions and professional recognition.
- **Interests & References**: Personal hobbies and professional recommendations.

## 2. Detailed Accomplishments (Highlights)
Go beyond simple job titles and bullet points. Every work experience and project allows you to attach **Highlights**—granular achievements that showcase your impact and measurable results.

[![Watch Highlights Walkthrough](https://img.youtube.com/vi/gMlDukvyFZs/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gMlDukvyFZs)

## 3. Section Visibility & Custom Ordering
Take complete control of how your resume structure is presented:
- **Section Ordering**: Arrange sections (Work Experience, Education, Skills, Volunteer, Projects, etc.) in the exact order that highlights your strongest assets first.
- **Section Visibility**: Instantly toggle individual sections on or off depending on the specific role or audience you are targeting.

## 4. Modular Customization & Themes
Organize your resume sections effortlessly and tailor the look and feel using multiple built-in professional themes:
- **Default, GitHub, Bold, Elegant, Terminal, Professional, PDF Optimized, and Blank** themes.

[![Watch Themes Walkthrough](https://img.youtube.com/vi/Qv1vk8l-LG0/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qv1vk8l-LG0)

---

## 5. Cover Letter Management
Craft, preview, and manage targeted cover letters right inside your dashboard alongside your resume profile.

---

## 6. Versatile Exports & JSON Resume Standard
- **PDF Export**: Generate beautifully formatted PDF resumes powered by Spatie Browsershot / Dompdf.
- **JSON Resume Export & Import**: Fully compatible with the [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org/) standard, making data portability seamless.
- **Public Downloads**: Optionally enable public download links for your PDF and JSON resume versions.

[![Watch Export & Import Walkthrough](https://img.youtube.com/vi/jnCrBUrOX2k/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jnCrBUrOX2k)

---

## 7. Open Graph Management
Ensure your professional profile makes a stunning impression when shared on social media (LinkedIn, X, Slack, etc.) with custom Open Graph image and metadata controls.
